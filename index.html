Sheet 1 · Chemical Composition & Initial Characterization

Record the chemical composition (wt.%) and the initial (as-cast) Rockwell hardness of each untreated specimen. The initial hardness provides the baseline for % reduction calculations.

Sample ID C (%) Si (%) Mn (%) S (%) P (%) Other Elements Initial Hardness Microstructure Description

Sheet 2 · Softening Heat Treatment

Three hardness readings (H1, H2, H3) are taken per specimen. Mean Hardness and % Hardness Reduction are calculated automatically — reduction is measured against the average initial hardness from Sheet 1.

Sample ID Treatment Temperature (°C) Holding Time (min) Cooling Condition H1 H2 H3 Mean Hardness % Hardness Reduction Microstructure Observation

Sheet 3 · Re-Hardening

Three hardness readings (H1, H2, H3) are taken per specimen. Mean Hardness and % Increase from Softened are calculated automatically — the increase is measured against the mean softened hardness from Sheet 2.

Sample ID Treatment Temperature (°C) Holding Time (min) Cooling Condition H1 H2 H3 Mean Hardness % Increase from Softened Microstructure Observation

Sheet 4 · Microstructure

Record the observed microstructure features and provide a micrograph file number. Use one row per specimen/condition examined.

Sample ID Condition Magnification Graphite Morphology Graphite Distribution Matrix Structure Visible Defects / Cracks Micrograph File No. Remarks

Formulas & Reference

Every formula used by the automatic calculators and chart generators in this sheet.
Notation: n = number of values; xi = individual value; x̄ = mean; s = sample standard deviation; k = number of values in a group; yi = group value.

① Mean Hardness Auto-calculated

Mean of 3 readings Sheets 2 & 3
x̄ = H1 + H2 + H3 3
Blank if no valid reading has been entered.

② % Hardness Reduction Sheet 2

Reduction relative to initial Auto
Reduction (%) = H̄initial − H̄softened H̄initial × 100
H̄initial = mean initial hardness of U-1, U-2, U-3 (Sheet 1). Positive value = softening (hardness dropped).

③ % Increase from Softened Sheet 3

Increase relative to softened Auto
Increase (%) = H̄re-hardened − H̄softened H̄softened × 100
H̄softened = mean of all valid mean hardness values from Sheet 2. Positive value = hardness recovered / increased after re-hardening.

④ Group Statistics Bar charts

Group mean Bar height
ȳ = 1k · Σi=1k yi
k = number of specimens in the group.
Group standard deviation Error bar
s = √ Σi=1k (yi − ȳ)² k − 1
Sample standard deviation (n−1).

⑤ Carbon Equivalent Sheet 1

Carbon equivalent (CE) Composition
CE = %C + %Si3
Used for chart ⑦ (composition vs. initial hardness). Eutectic ≈ 4.3 %.

⑥ Linear Regression Trend line

Summations
Sx = Σ xi  |  Sy = Σ yi  |  Sxx = Σ xi²  |  Sxy = Σ xiyi  |  Syy = Σ yi²
Slope m
m = n·Sxy − Sx·Sy n·Sxx − (Sx)²
Intercept b
b = Sy − m·Sx n
Coefficient of determination R²
R² = (n·Sxy − Sx·Sy)² (n·Sxx − Sx²)(n·Syy − Sy²)
Trend line equation Displayed on chart
y = m·x + b

⑦ Axis Scaling Nice-number algorithm

Step size
step = niceNum( range / (ticks − 1), round )
niceNum snaps to 1, 2, 5, or 10 × 10k.
Axis limits
minaxis = ⌊ mindata / step ⌋ · step
maxaxis = ⌈ maxdata / step ⌉ · step

Instructions & Notes

ItemInstruction
PurposeThis workbook records chemical composition, hardness and microstructural observations only. No machining data is collected.
Sheet 1Record the chemical composition (C, Si, Mn, S, P, other elements in wt.%) and the initial (as-cast) hardness of U-1, U-2, U-3.
Sheet 2Record softening heat treatment (temperature, holding time, cooling condition) and three hardness readings (H1, H2, H3) per specimen. Mean hardness and % hardness reduction are calculated automatically against the average initial hardness.
Sheet 3Record re-hardening parameters and three hardness readings per specimen. Mean hardness and % increase from softened are calculated automatically against the mean softened hardness.
Sheet 4Record observed microstructure features (magnification, graphite morphology, distribution, matrix structure, defects) and provide a micrograph file number.
HardnessUse the same test method and procedure for all comparable specimens. Record hardness on the same scale throughout.
Heat treatmentEnter the actual furnace temperature and holding time used in the experiment.
GraphsThe Graphs tab plots softening / re-hardening temperature vs. hardness, treatment stage vs. hardness, cooling condition, % reduction, % increase, and composition vs. initial hardness.
