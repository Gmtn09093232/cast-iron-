Formulas & Reference

Every formula used by the automatic calculators and chart generators in this sheet.
Notation: n = number of values; xi = individual value; x̄ = mean; s = sample standard deviation; k = number of values in a group; yi = group value.

① Baseline Reference Auto

Baseline hardness Reference
Baseline = Hardness of S01 (as-cast)
Used for all percentage-change calculations. If S01 has no hardness value, the first specimen with a recorded hardness whose Initial Condition is As-cast is used instead.
Hardness change (%) Reduction / Recovery
Change (%) = HRCi − Baseline Baseline × 100
Negative → softening (reduction). Positive → hardening / recovery.

② Group Statistics Bar charts

Group mean Bar height
ȳ = 1 k · Σi=1k yi
k = number of specimens in the group (e.g. all specimens sharing the same cooling method).
Group standard deviation Error bar
s = √ Σi=1k (yi − ȳ)² k − 1
Sample standard deviation (n−1). If k = 1, the deviation is set to 0.
Error bar Bar chart whiskers
Error bar = ȳ ± s

③ Linear Regression Trend line

Applied to the scatter charts (heating temp, re-hardening temp, holding time vs. hardness). Given n data points (xi, yi):

Summations
Sx = Σ xi  |  Sy = Σ yi  |  Sxx = Σ xi²  |  Sxy = Σ xiyi  |  Syy = Σ yi²
Slope m
m = n·Sxy − Sx·Sy n·Sxx − (Sx)²
Intercept b
b = Sy − m·Sx n
Correlation coefficient r
r = n·Sxy − Sx·Sy √[ (n·Sxx − Sx²)(n·Syy − Sy²) ]
Coefficient of determination R²
R² = r²
Trend line equation Displayed on chart
y = m·x + b

④ Axis Scaling Nice-number algorithm

Cosmetic only — rounds axis limits and tick steps to human-friendly numbers.

Step size
step = niceNum( range / (ticks − 1), round )
niceNum snaps to 1, 2, 5, or 10 × 10k.
Axis limits
minaxis = ⌊ mindata / step ⌋ · step
maxaxis = ⌈ maxdata / step ⌉ · step

Instructions & Notes

ItemInstruction
PurposeThis sheet records hardness and microstructural observations only.
MachinabilityDo NOT collect machining time, cutting force, tool wear, surface roughness, cutting speed, feed, or similar machining data. Machinability is interpreted from hardness changes as defined in the study.
HardnessRecord Rockwell hardness (HRC) after each required treatment condition. Use the same test method and procedure for all comparable specimens.
MicrostructureRecord the observed phases/features and provide an image / file reference where available.
Heat treatmentEnter the actual furnace temperature and holding time used in the experiment.
CoolingRecord the actual cooling method used (e.g. furnace cooling, air cooling, water quenching, oil quenching).
Specimen IDsPre-filled S01–S08 follow the study matrix. Extra blank rows (S09–S12) are available for additional specimens.
GraphsThe “Graphs & Relations” tab plots heating/re-hardening temperature, holding time, cooling method, initial condition and heat treatment against hardness, plus the % hardness change relative to the as-cast baseline.
